## THE TWIN MIND INTERFACE

Neuroveil explores a feedback loop between two people: OpenBCI headsets capture EEG, ESP32-connected glasses turn selected frequencies into light, and a cymatics setup turns the signal into visible patterns. The project lives in the Synchroflow repository.

The AI component used EEG recordings from team members to train a custom model that identifies whose data a sample most resembles. Inference ran on the Qualcomm RB3 Gen 2. [Qualcomm's project feature](https://www.qualcomm.com/developer/project/neuroveil--twin-mind-interface) documents the hardware and model workflow.
